IBM TRIRIGA® software vs IBM Maximo® software: what changes for lease data

IBM describes Maximo® Real Estate and Facilities as the evolution of IBM TRIRIGA®, and as the designated upgrade path before TRIRIGA support ends. This page sets out what each system is, how lease, space and facilities data are handled, and what to check when the records move.

Published November 2026 · About a seven minute read

What each system is

IBM TRIRIGA® is the integrated workplace management system many organisations have used for real estate and facilities work, including leases, space and maintenance. IBM now describes IBM Maximo® Real Estate and Facilities, often shortened to Maximo REF, as formerly IBM TRIRIGA, and as the evolution of TRIRIGA.[1][2]

Maximo Real Estate and Facilities 9.x sits inside IBM Maximo Application Suite (MAS) 9.x. IBM's support lifecycle notice names that offering as the designated upgrade path for TRIRIGA.[3]

A useful distinction

A search for TRIRIGA vs Maximo can mix up two different things. Maximo Real Estate and Facilities is the TRIRIGA successor. IBM Maximo Application Suite is the broader platform that now includes it. This page is about the move from TRIRIGA to Maximo REF.

Lease management, space and facilities

IBM's documentation for Maximo Real Estate and Facilities describes the same domains TRIRIGA customers already work in, now under the Maximo REF name and on the Maximo Application Suite platform.[1]

Lease administration
IBM describes automated tracking of payment streams and invoice validation against contract terms, aimed at streamlining property management and reducing overpayments or penalties.
Lease accounting
IBM describes integrated lease lifecycle data management, accounting treatment and calculations, journal entries, and disclosure reporting in line with GAAP and IFRS.
Space
Space allocation, moves and audits, utilisation tracking, and space use agreements, used to manage how space is assigned and occupied.
Facilities
Facility condition, capital projects, maintenance, reservations and related workplace functions sit alongside the lease and space records on the same platform.

IBM also publishes a practical difference for the upgrade itself. The application upgrade path depends on whether you use the Lease module, and on your source version. After the move to Maximo REF Platform 9.2, customers who do not use the Lease module can often complete the application upgrade in a single step. Customers who use the Lease module may complete it in one step or in several, depending on the source version and on Patch Helper dependencies.[4] IBM's documentation gives an example already discussed on our lease data migration checklist: moving from application 10.8 can take two steps, first to 11.2, then to 9.2.

Why organisations move from TRIRIGA to Maximo REF

This is not a bake off between two unrelated products. IBM has set a support lifecycle for TRIRIGA, and has named Maximo REF as the path forward.

  • Support for all versions and releases of IBM TRIRIGA software and the IBM TRIRIGA Application Suite (TAS) ends on 30 September 2027.
  • New software orders, including TRIRIGA SaaS, closed on 28 January 2026.
  • IBM Maximo Real Estate and Facilities 9.x, part of IBM Maximo Application Suite 9.x, is the designated upgrade path.
  • Customers who stay on TRIRIGA or TAS after September 2027 can move to IBM Sustained Support for up to five years, with chargeable Extended Support available for the first 12 months.
  • Customers who trade up to MAS get dual support for up to 19 months beyond the base support end date. IBM states that dual support under the MAS licence ends on 30 April 2029.

Dates per IBM's published support lifecycle notice. Confirm against IBM's own pages before relying on them for planning.[3]

What lease data has to be checked or mapped

A migration proves that a value moved from one field to another. It proves nothing about whether the value was ever right. The records that matter most are the ones that drive ASC 842, GASB 87 or SFFAS 54 numbers, and the operational dates around them.

Critical dates
Commencement, expiry, rent commencement and any other date that starts a payment or a notice window. Check them against the lease and any commencement memorandum, not against the date the record was created.
Options
Extension, termination and renewal options, with their notice periods. Under GASB 87 and ASC 842, options that are reasonably certain to be exercised, or not exercised, change the recognised term and the liability.
Payment schedules
Base rent, step increases, abatements and free rent periods, each tied to the clause that sets them. Variable payments, including index linked rent and operating cost pass throughs, should be kept distinct from fixed payments.
GASB 87 and ASC 842 inputs
The term used to discount the liability, the basis of the discount rate, residual value guarantees, covenants and restrictions, subleases, and leases not yet commenced. These often live in the body of the lease, not in a single structured field.

Questions we are asked

Is Maximo Real Estate and Facilities the same as TRIRIGA?
IBM describes Maximo Real Estate and Facilities as formerly IBM TRIRIGA, and as the evolution of TRIRIGA. It is the designated upgrade path for TRIRIGA customers, now delivered as part of IBM Maximo Application Suite.
Why are organisations moving from TRIRIGA to Maximo REF?
IBM has announced that support for all versions and releases of IBM TRIRIGA software and the IBM TRIRIGA Application Suite ends on 30 September 2027, and that new software orders closed on 28 January 2026. IBM names Maximo Real Estate and Facilities 9.x, part of Maximo Application Suite, as the designated upgrade path.
How is lease management handled in Maximo REF?
IBM's documentation for Maximo Real Estate and Facilities describes lease administration, including tracking of payment streams and invoice validation against contract terms, and lease accounting, including lifecycle data, calculations, journal entries and disclosure reporting in line with GAAP and IFRS. Because Maximo REF is the evolution of TRIRIGA, these are the same domains TRIRIGA lease teams already work in.
What lease data should be checked before a TRIRIGA to Maximo REF migration?
Start with the values that move the accounting numbers: commencement and other critical dates, the lease term and options, payment schedules, and the inputs used for ASC 842, GASB 87 or SFFAS 54. A migration proves that a value moved. It does not prove the value was ever right.
